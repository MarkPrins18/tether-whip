Tether Whip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Tether Whip is a casual mobile game. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle. The developer is Mark Prins (Netherlands).

What stays on your device

Your scores, coins, upgrades, skins, missions and settings are stored only on your phone. The developer does not run a server for the game, has no user accounts, and does not receive this data. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. The developer does not receive this personal data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice later in Settings under "Ad privacy choices" when it is shown. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Permissions

The game uses the internet connection to load ads and the vibration function for haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Your rights

Because the developer does not hold your personal data, there is nothing to export or delete on our side. For data held by Google, use the controls described above or in your Google account.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game changes. The latest version is always on this page. Questions can be raised at github.com/MarkPrins18/tether-whip/issues.
