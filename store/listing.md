**Full description:**
Hold to swing. Let go to fly. Outrun the void.

Tether Whip is a fast one-thumb arcade game. Grab an anchor, spin faster as the rope reels in, then release at the perfect moment to rocket toward the next one.

- Perfect releases: when the guide line turns green, let go for a speed boost and bonus points.
- Chain orbs to build a score multiplier up to x6.
- Slip past mines for close-call bonuses, grab shields, and collect stars.
- Five sectors with their own look, from the quiet Basin to the glowing Zenith.
- Upgrades, skins and missions that keep every run worth one more try.
- Runs last a minute or two. Play anywhere, no account needed.
